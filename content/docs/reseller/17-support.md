## What Support is for

If something does not work, you are stuck, a bill looks wrong or you want to ask a question, open a ticket. A ticket is a conversation between you and the myBoxStock team that stays in one place, with a number such as T-1042, so nothing gets lost in an inbox.

Open it from the **Support** item, the last item in the left menu after Documentation (on a phone or tablet it is in the **More** sheet). You can also click your name at the top right (on a phone, your name at the top of the screen) and choose **Support** in the **account menu**. Everyone who can sign in to the account can open tickets, whether they are an Administrator, a Standard user or a View user. Support also still works when a trial has ended or an account is closing, because that is when people most need to ask.

## What the team can read

This matters, so read it once. Your inventory, customers and sales are encrypted with your own key, and nobody at myBoxStock can read them. A ticket is different: it is plain text that you send to the team on purpose, so the team can read it. For that reason:

- Do not paste customer details, passwords or your recovery key into a ticket. The form reminds you.
- Screenshots are read too. Hide anything private before you attach one.
- Closed tickets are kept until the owner of the site removes them. They are not deleted when you close one.

The team never needs your password or recovery key, and will never ask for them. If someone does, do not send them. See [Your data, your responsibility](#/docs/your-data-your-responsibility).

## My tickets

The Support page lists your tickets, newest activity first, with a status. Administrators see **Tickets for this account**: every ticket anyone in the account opened, with an **Opened by** column. Standard and View users see only the tickets they opened themselves.

The status says whose move it is:

- **Open**: you have sent it and the team has not answered yet.
- **Waiting on myBoxStock**: the team has it.
- **Waiting on you**: the team asked something or replied, and is waiting for you.
- **Resolved**: the team believes it is sorted. If it is not, reply and it opens again.
- **Closed**: finished. You can read it, but not reply. Open a new ticket and mention the old number if you need more help.

A Resolved ticket closes by itself after a few days without a reply.

## Opening a ticket

Choose **New ticket**.

1. Pick a **Category**: Question, Problem, Billing, Backup and restore or Account. The site's Host can change this list.
2. Write a short **Subject**, 3 to 120 characters, that says what it is about.
3. Write the **Message**. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened. Mention the device and browser if it matters.
4. Optionally use **Add screenshot** to attach pictures. Only PNG or JPG files are accepted, up to a size limit shown beside the button, and a small number per message.
5. Optionally use **Attach diagnostics**. See below.
6. Choose **Send ticket**.

There are limits to stop floods: only a few new tickets per hour and a cap on how many can be open at once. If you reach one, the message says so. Reply to an existing ticket, or wait.

### Attach diagnostics

Diagnostics are off unless you switch them on. The button shows exactly what would be sent before you send it: your app version, plan, how many people the account has, whether encryption and two-factor are on, the date of your last backup, and recent warnings. It never includes inventory, customers or sales. It is the same summary as Copy diagnostics on [Backup and restore](#/docs/backup-and-restore), and it often saves a round of questions. Choose **Remove diagnostics** if you change your mind.

## Following a ticket

When the team replies, a red number appears beside your name in the top bar, beside **Support** in the account menu and beside **Support** in the left menu. On a phone or tablet it also shows on the **More** button, so you can see a reply without opening the sheet. The Support list marks the ticket with **New reply**. Open it to read the **Conversation**: your messages, and the team's replies shaded blue. The number clears once you have read the ticket.

If the site has email set up and your account has an email address, you are also emailed that there is a reply. The email does not contain the reply, only the ticket number and a button to open the app. If you do not get emails, the red number still tells you.

To answer, type in the **Reply** box and choose **Send reply**. Screenshots and diagnostics can be attached to replies too. Replying to a Resolved ticket opens it again.

## Looking at a screenshot

Click or tap a screenshot in a conversation, or the small preview of a picture you are about to send, and it opens in the **Screenshot viewer**, a window on top of the ticket. Nothing leaves the page. The picture is scaled to fit the window; tap or click it once to see it at full size and again to fit it back.

- **Close**, the Escape key, a tap outside the window and the back gesture on a phone all close the viewer and return you to the same place in the ticket.
- When one message has several screenshots, **Previous** and **Next** move between them and a count such as "2 of 3" shows where you are. The left and right arrow keys work too.
- **Download** saves the picture to your device.
- **Open in new tab** (a small link) opens the raw picture in a browser tab, in case you want that. It is secondary on purpose, because the viewer is the normal way.

## Tips for quick answers

- One question per ticket.
- Include the exact message you saw on screen.
- Say whether it happens on a phone, tablet or computer.
- Attach a screenshot of the problem, with private details hidden.
- Attach diagnostics for anything about backups, sign-in, Team or something that stopped working.

> If you cannot sign in at all, you cannot open a ticket. Ask your account administrator for help first, and see [Security](#/docs/security) and [Troubleshooting and FAQ](#/docs/troubleshooting-faq).
