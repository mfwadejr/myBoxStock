## What this page is for

myBoxStock is meant to be usable by everyone on your team, on a phone, a tablet or a computer: with a mouse, a finger, only a keyboard, a screen reader, larger text or the reduce-motion setting turned on. This page says what to expect and how to tell us when something falls short.

## Using only the keyboard

Every button, link, switch, check box and field can be reached with **Tab** (and **Shift + Tab** to go back). The control you are on shows a clear blue outline, the **focus ring**, and it looks the same everywhere in the app.

- **Enter** or **Space** presses the control you are on, including a switch or a check box.
- **Escape** closes a window (a sheet), the account menu, or an open dropdown, whichever is on top. When a window closes, you land back on the button that opened it.
- When a window opens, the cursor moves into it, and **Tab** stays inside until you close it, so you never wander off behind it.
- In a dropdown, **Enter**, **Space** or the **Down arrow** opens the list. The arrow keys, **Home** and **End** move through the choices, typing a letter jumps to a choice that starts with it, and **Enter** picks it.
- In the account menu, the arrow keys move between items, and **Escape** closes it.
- A row in a table that opens something (a ticket, a sale, a person) can be reached with **Tab** and opened with **Enter** or **Space**.
- On [Customers](#/docs/customers), press **/** (or **Ctrl + K**, **Cmd + K** on a Mac) to jump to the search box, and **Escape** to clear it.
- When you reorder a list in [Settings](#/docs/settings), focus the grip, press **Space** to pick the row up, move it with the arrow keys, **Space** again to drop it, or **Escape** to cancel.
- In the screenshot viewer on [Support](#/docs/support), the **Left** and **Right** arrow keys move between pictures and **Escape** closes it.
- In the camera scanner on [Scanning with your phone](#/docs/scanning-with-your-phone), **Escape** closes it, and **Type it instead** lets you key the code in.

## Screen readers

Every control has a name that a screen reader reads out: fields are tied to their labels, buttons that show only an icon have a spoken name, and switches are named after the setting they change. Segmented buttons and filters say whether they are pressed. The menu button says whether the menu is open, and the page you are on in the menu is marked as current.

Messages that appear and fade ("Saved", "Could not save") are read out politely without moving your place on the page, and errors are read out right away. A new count in the menu, such as replies waiting on [Support](#/docs/support), is announced once when it goes up. Long jobs report each step and each quarter of the way, not every second.

## Colour, size and motion

- Text and buttons meet the WCAG 2.1 AA contrast level (at least 4.5 to 1 for text). Where we found colours that were too faint, they were darkened in this release.
- A status is never shown by colour alone. A red, amber or green chip always carries a word, such as **Overdue** or **Trial ended**.
- On phones and tablets every button, tab and field is at least 44 pixels high, so it is comfortable to tap, and the fields are large enough that the phone does not zoom in by itself.
- You can zoom the page or enlarge the text in your browser, and the layout reflows without sideways scrolling.
- If your device is set to **Reduce motion**, myBoxStock turns off its slide, fade and progress animations.

## Report an accessibility problem

If something cannot be reached, read or used the way this page describes, please tell us.

1. Open **Support** (in the account menu at the top, or in the left menu) and choose **New ticket**.
2. Pick the **Category** **Problem** and start the **Subject** with the word Accessibility.
3. In the **Message**, say which screen, what you were trying to do, what happened, and what you use (for example VoiceOver on an iPhone, or a keyboard only on a laptop).
4. Choose **Send ticket**. Please do not include customer details or passwords. See [Support](#/docs/support).

> Short of a ticket, tell your account administrator, who can open one for you.
