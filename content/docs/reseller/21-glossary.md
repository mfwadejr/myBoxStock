Words are listed in alphabetical order. A word in bold inside a definition has its own entry.

### Account

Your business's space in myBoxStock. It holds your team, your inventory, your customers and your sales, all under one **Reseller ID**. See [Getting started](#/docs/getting-started).

### Account menu

The button with your username at the top right of every page. It opens a small menu with your name, your user type and your business, then **Site admin** (only for accounts linked to a site administrator) and **Sign out**. It closes when you tap outside it, press Escape or choose an item. On a phone it rises from the bottom. See [Getting started](#/docs/getting-started).

### Activity

A page, for Administrators, listing who signed in to the account, from where, on which device, and which devices are signed in right now. It also lets you sign people out. See [Activity](#/docs/activity).

### Add what is missing

The safe choice when you restore a backup file. It adds records that are in the file but not in your account, and changes nothing that is already there. Compare **Replace everything**. See [Backup and restore](#/docs/backup-and-restore).

### Administrator

The user type that can do everything: manage the team, change settings, see activity, create recovery keys, export all data and close the account. An account always has at least one. See [Team](#/docs/team).

### Aim line

The thin line across the middle of the scan box on the camera scanner. When a screen shows several barcodes, the one nearest the aim line is the one that is read. It moves with the scan box when you tap the picture (see Tap to aim), and turns green when a code is read. See [Scanning with your phone camera](#/docs/scanning-with-your-phone).

### Archive

Hides a device from normal lists without deleting it. An archived device can be restored later. See [Inventory](#/docs/inventory).

### Authenticator

An app on your phone (such as 1Password, Google Authenticator or Authy) that shows a 6 digit code which changes every half minute. You type the code at sign-in when **two-factor** is on. See [Security](#/docs/security).

### Available

A device status meaning it is in stock and can be sold. A device marked **Returned** can also be sold again. See [Inventory](#/docs/inventory).

### Awaiting test

A status shown for a device that still has required test steps to tick when your account requires devices to be tested. It cannot be sold until the steps are done. See [Inventory](#/docs/inventory).

### Backup file

A file ending in `.mbsbackup` that holds a copy of your whole account, still locked with your key. You make it with **Back up now** and keep it somewhere safe. It opens only with your password or recovery key and restores only into its own account. It also holds your team list (usernames, emails and user types, never passwords). See [Backup and restore](#/docs/backup-and-restore).

### Bulk scan

A way to add many devices at once. You choose what is the same for all of them (make, model, cost and so on), then scan each device's label one after another, pressing Enter after each. See [Inventory](#/docs/inventory).

### Carrier

The company that delivers a parcel, such as USPS or UPS. You can type it on a Shipping sale. It prints on the label only when entered. See [Delivery and shipping](#/docs/delivery-and-shipping).

### Catalog

The list of makes and models you sell, kept in Settings and used to fill in devices quickly. Models can also have a **reorder level**. See [Settings](#/docs/settings).

### Confirm each scan

A choice on the camera scanner, off by default and remembered on that device only. When it is on, a code that has been read is shown with **Use this** and **Scan again**, and nothing is filled in until you tap **Use this**. It also works in Bulk scan and for the MAC address and UID steps. See [Scanning with your phone camera](#/docs/scanning-with-your-phone).

### Cost

What you paid for a device. It is used to work out profit on Home and Sales, and is saved on each sale. See [Inventory](#/docs/inventory).

### Credit note

The refund receipt a customer gets after a return: the devices refunded, any restocking fee, the total refunded and how it was paid. It never shows your costs. See [Returns and refunds](#/docs/returns-and-refunds).

### Customer

A person you sell to, with a name, phone, email and notes, and a list of their purchases. Every sale has a customer. See [Customers](#/docs/customers).

### Date received

The day a device came into your stock. See [Inventory](#/docs/inventory).

### Delivery

How a sale reaches the customer: **Immediate**, **Shipping**, **Pickup** or **Meet**. Chosen on Quick sale. Older sales read as Immediate. See [Delivery and shipping](#/docs/delivery-and-shipping).

### Delivery note

A short instruction for the carrier, such as "Fragile, leave at the door". It prints on the shipping label under the heading Delivery note, and on the receipt only if you switch that on in Settings. See [Shipping labels](#/docs/shipping-labels).

### DEMO chip

A small amber **DEMO** tag and banner. It appears only when you are signed in to a demonstration account made by the site's Host, which holds made-up data and never sends email. Real accounts never show it. See [Getting started](#/docs/getting-started).

### Diagnostics

A short plain-text summary of your account's health that **Copy diagnostics** puts on your clipboard, to paste to your site's administrator. It has versions, counts and recent warnings, and never your devices, customers or sales. See [Backup and restore](#/docs/backup-and-restore).

### Disabled

A person whose sign-in has been switched off by an Administrator. They cannot sign in, and are signed out everywhere, until it is switched back on. See [Team](#/docs/team).

### Discount

A percentage taken off one device, or off the whole order, in **Quick sale**. Standard users can give discounts only up to a limit set in Settings. See [Quick sale](#/docs/quick-sale).

### Drag handle

The six grey dots at the end of a row in Settings when its **Reorder** switch is on. Drag it to move the row, or focus it and use Space and the arrow keys. See [Settings](#/docs/settings).

### Encryption

Scrambling your inventory, customers and sales in your browser so that only people with your account's key can read them. The hosting service stores the scrambled version and cannot read it. See [Your data, your responsibility](#/docs/your-data-your-responsibility).

### Erase

Removes a customer's personal details (name, phone, email, notes) from their record and from their past sales, keeping totals and receipt numbers. It cannot be undone. See [Customers](#/docs/customers).

### Export

Downloading your data as ordinary files. This is not a backup you can restore from; for that, make a **backup file**. **Export everything** on Security gives one file of spreadsheets and settings built in your browser; Inventory, Sales and individual customers can be exported too. See [Security](#/docs/security) and [Your data, your responsibility](#/docs/your-data-your-responsibility).

### Focus ring

The clear blue outline around the control you are on when you move with the keyboard. It looks the same everywhere. See [Accessibility](#/docs/accessibility).

### Free trial

The period at the start of an account in which you can use myBoxStock without paying. A chip at the top shows the days left. When it ends, the account becomes **read-only**. See [Plans, trials and billing](#/docs/plans-trials-billing).

### Get set up card

The checklist at the top of Home for Administrators: first device, payment methods, first sale, team member (optional), first backup, a tested backup file and your recovery key. It hides when done or when dismissed. More in [Home](#/docs/home).

### Idle lock

An optional Settings choice that locks your data after a number of minutes without activity, when the account is set to stay unlocked after a refresh. See [Settings](#/docs/settings).

### Import

Adding devices in bulk from a CSV file on the Inventory page. Rows without an identifier, or with one that already exists, are skipped. See [Inventory](#/docs/inventory).

### Inventory

Your list of devices, with status, cost, price, test results and the other details you track. See [Inventory](#/docs/inventory).

### Label logo

The picture printed on a shipping label: your business logo, a different one for labels, or none. Chosen in Settings and changeable on a single label. See [Shipping labels](#/docs/shipping-labels).

### Locked (sign-in)

What happens for 15 minutes after six wrong passwords in a row for the same sign-in. The person who runs the site can unlock it earlier; they never see or change your password when they do. See [Troubleshooting and FAQ](#/docs/troubleshooting-faq).

### Look up in sale

A setting on each device detail (such as UID, serial number or MAC) saying that Quick sale may use it to find the device when scanned or typed. See [Settings](#/docs/settings).

### MAC address

A device identifier made of letters and numbers that some devices have. You can track it as a detail and scan or type it in Quick sale if it is marked **Look up in sale**. See [Settings](#/docs/settings).

### Make and model

The brand and the product name of a device. They are kept in the **catalog**. See [Settings](#/docs/settings).

### Mark shipped

The button that records that a parcel has gone. It asks for an optional date, carrier and tracking number and moves the sale from **To ship** to **Shipped**. See [Delivery and shipping](#/docs/delivery-and-shipping).

### Meet

A delivery choice for handing a device over somewhere else. It records the place, time and who was there as a note; no address is needed. See [Delivery and shipping](#/docs/delivery-and-shipping).

### Order number

The receipt number of a sale, such as `S-20261009-AB3`. It is always printed on a shipping label, with the date. See [Shipping labels](#/docs/shipping-labels) and **Receipt number**.

### Paid by

The payment method recorded on a sale, such as cash or card. Payment methods are set in Settings. See [Quick sale](#/docs/quick-sale).

### Partly returned

An amber tag on a sale where some of the devices came back and others did not. A sale where every device came back shows a grey **Returned** tag instead. See [Returns and refunds](#/docs/returns-and-refunds).

### Password

Your secret for signing in. It must be at least 10 characters with letters and numbers. In an encrypted account it also protects the key to your data. See [Security](#/docs/security).

### Pending invitation

A team member who came back from a backup file and has not been given a password yet. They show on the [Team](#/docs/team) page with a **Pending invitation** tag and cannot sign in until an Administrator presses **Set up access** and gives them a temporary password. They then choose their own password and set up their own two-factor.

### Pickup

A delivery choice for a customer who collects the device from you later. It records an optional date and note. See [Delivery and shipping](#/docs/delivery-and-shipping).

### Preset (scan box size)

The three sizes of the scan box on the camera scanner: **Small**, **Medium** and **Large**. Your choice is remembered on that device. See [Scanning with your phone camera](#/docs/scanning-with-your-phone).

### Quick sale

The page for recording a sale in one go: scan or type devices, set prices, choose a customer, and finish. See [Quick sale](#/docs/quick-sale).

### Read-only

The state of an account whose trial or paid period has ended, or which is closing. You can look at and export everything but cannot add or change anything. Nothing is deleted. See [Plans, trials and billing](#/docs/plans-trials-billing).

### Receipt

The record of a sale, with a number like S-20261004-7K2Q, the devices, the total, how it was paid and the warranty. It can be printed or emailed. See [Sales](#/docs/sales).

### Receipt number

The unique code on every sale. It starts with S, then the sale date, then five random letters and digits. See [Sales](#/docs/sales).

### Recovery code

One of a set of single-use codes shown when you turn on **two-factor**. Use one in place of the 6 digit code if you lose your phone. See [Security](#/docs/security).

### Recovery key

A long code shown once when encryption is turned on. It is the only way to get your data back if everyone forgets their passwords. The hosting service cannot see or recover it. An Administrator can create a new one, which cancels the old one. See [Your data, your responsibility](#/docs/your-data-your-responsibility).

### Refund

Money you give back to a customer after a return. The app records it (amount, reason and how it was paid) but never sends money. See [Returns and refunds](#/docs/returns-and-refunds).

### Reorder level

A number on a model. When the available devices of that model fall to it or below, Home shows a low-stock notice. See [Inventory](#/docs/inventory).

### Reorder switch

A switch at the top of the Settings cards for device details, warranty periods, payment methods and test steps. Off (the normal state) locks the rows in place; on shows a **drag handle** on each row so you can change the order. Turning it off locks the new order in. Not the same as a **reorder level**, which is a stock number on a model. See [Settings](#/docs/settings).

### Replace everything

The stronger choice when you restore a backup file. It makes your account match the file exactly: it overwrites changed records, adds missing ones and deletes records that are not in the file. A safety copy lets you undo for 7 days. See [Backup and restore](#/docs/backup-and-restore).

### Required before sale

A test step marked so that a device is not meant to be sold until it has been ticked. You can still choose to sell anyway, and the sale records what was missed. See [Settings](#/docs/settings).

### Reseller ID

The code that identifies your account, for example `amber-fox-4271`. Everyone on your team signs in with the same Reseller ID and their own username. See [Getting started](#/docs/getting-started).

### Reserved

A device status for a device set aside for someone. It is not offered in Quick sale until it is made available. See [Inventory](#/docs/inventory).

### Reset access

An Administrator's way of helping a teammate who cannot sign in: it gives them a temporary password and signs them out, and they choose their own at next sign-in. See [Team](#/docs/team).

### Restocking fee

An amount you keep when a customer returns a device, taken off the refund. It starts at none and can be set as a default in Settings. See [Returns and refunds](#/docs/returns-and-refunds).

### Restore point

The safety copy the site keeps, locked, just before you restore a backup file. It lasts 7 days and is what **Undo last restore** puts back. There is only one at a time. See [Backup and restore](#/docs/backup-and-restore).

### Return address

The address printed at the top right of a shipping label. Set in Settings, and changeable for a single print. See [Shipping labels](#/docs/shipping-labels).

### Return or refund

The button on a sale's receipt that records a customer sending devices back and the refund given. Void is different: it only fixes a sale that should not have happened. See [Returns and refunds](#/docs/returns-and-refunds).

### Return reason

A short reason chosen when you process a return, such as "Faulty". The list is yours to edit in Settings under Returns and refunds. See [Settings](#/docs/settings).

### Return requested

A blue tag on a sale. A Standard user asked for a refund above the limit an Administrator set, so an Administrator needs to open the sale and process it. See [Returns and refunds](#/docs/returns-and-refunds).

### Returned

A device status for a device that came back from a customer. It can be sold again. Processing a return on a sale lets you choose it. See [Inventory](#/docs/inventory) and [Returns and refunds](#/docs/returns-and-refunds).

### Running low

The card on Home that lists, model by model, the stock that has dropped to its reorder level, with Out of stock shown separately from Low. See [Home](#/docs/home).

### Sale

One transaction, with one or more devices, one customer and one receipt. See [Sales](#/docs/sales).

### Saved address

The delivery address kept on a customer so that **Ship to** fills in by itself on their next shipped sale. Edit or clear it on the customer's page. See [Customers](#/docs/customers) and [Delivery and shipping](#/docs/delivery-and-shipping).

### Scan box

The bright rectangle on the camera scanner. Only the part of the picture inside it is read. Tap the picture to move it onto the barcode you want. See [Scanning with your phone camera](#/docs/scanning-with-your-phone).

### Screenshot viewer

The window on top of a support ticket that shows a picture at full size, with Previous, Next, Download and Open in new tab. See [Support](#/docs/support).

### Selling price

The price you expect to charge for a device. In Quick sale it is the starting price, which you can change. See [Inventory](#/docs/inventory).

### Serial number

A manufacturer's number printed on a device. It can be tracked as a detail and used to find a device. See [Settings](#/docs/settings).

### Session

One signed-in device or browser. Sessions last up to 14 days, and you can see and end them on Security and Activity. See [Activity](#/docs/activity).

### Settings

An Administrator page for choosing which device details to track, the test checklist, makes and models, warranty periods, payment methods, discounts, unlock behaviour and email sending. See [Settings](#/docs/settings).

### Ship to

The name and address a parcel goes to. It fills in from the customer's saved address and prints large on the label. See [Delivery and shipping](#/docs/delivery-and-shipping).

### Shipped

The status of a Shipping sale after you press Mark shipped. See [Delivery and shipping](#/docs/delivery-and-shipping).

### Shipping

A delivery choice for sending a device by post or courier. The sale carries an address, a shipping fee and optional tracking. See [Delivery and shipping](#/docs/delivery-and-shipping).

### Shipping cost

What the postage actually cost you. It is internal: used for Profit after shipping on Sales and never shown on a receipt or label. See [Delivery and shipping](#/docs/delivery-and-shipping).

### Shipping fee

What you charge the customer for shipping. It is its own line on the receipt, kept apart from item prices, discounts and profit. See [Delivery and shipping](#/docs/delivery-and-shipping).

### Shipping label

A printable address label for a parcel in three sizes, with no barcode. See [Shipping labels](#/docs/shipping-labels).

### Standard

A user type that can work with inventory, customers and sales and record quick sales, but cannot manage the team, settings or activity. See [Team](#/docs/team).

### Status

Where a device is in its life: Available, Reserved, Sold, Returned, Awaiting test, Damaged or Archived. See [Inventory](#/docs/inventory).

### Support

The page, reached from the account menu, where you open and follow tickets with the myBoxStock team. Tickets can be read by the team, so never paste customer details, passwords or your recovery key. More in [Support](#/docs/support).

### Suspended

An account which the site operator has paused. Nobody can sign in until it is lifted. See [Plans, trials and billing](#/docs/plans-trials-billing).

### Tap to aim

Tapping the live camera picture on the scanner to move the scan box and its aim line to the barcode you want, instead of moving the phone. The box keeps its size and stays on the picture, and goes back to the middle the next time the scanner opens. See [Scanning with your phone camera](#/docs/scanning-with-your-phone).

### Team

The page where Administrators add people, choose their user types, disable them, reset their access or delete them. See [Team](#/docs/team).

### Test a backup file

A card on Backup and restore that checks a backup file in your browser without restoring anything: is it a backup, made for this account, does it open with this account's key, is it complete, can every record be opened. It also shows what is inside and how it compares with your account now. See [Backup and restore](#/docs/backup-and-restore).

### Test checklist

The list of steps (such as "powers on") that you tick for each device, set up in Settings. Steps can be marked **required before sale**. See [Settings](#/docs/settings).

### Tested on

The date a device was tested. It defaults to today. See [Inventory](#/docs/inventory).

### Themed and Plain

The two looks of a shipping label. **Themed** follows the look of the site. **Plain** is black on white with no tints, the best choice for a thermal label printer. See [Shipping labels](#/docs/shipping-labels).

### Ticket

A numbered conversation with the myBoxStock team, such as T-1042. Its status says whose move it is: Open, Waiting on myBoxStock, Waiting on you, Resolved or Closed. More in [Support](#/docs/support).

### To ship

The status of a Shipping sale that has not been marked shipped yet. Filter the Sales list by it to see parcels waiting to go out. See [Sales](#/docs/sales).

### Tracking number

The carrier's number for following a parcel. Optional; it can be added after the sale and prints on the label only when entered. See [Delivery and shipping](#/docs/delivery-and-shipping).

### Trial

See **Free trial**.

### Two-factor

A second step at sign-in: besides your password you type a code from your **authenticator**. It is optional and a good idea. See [Security](#/docs/security).

### UID

A device's unique identifier, usually on its label. It is the most common thing to scan. Which identifiers you track and can scan is set in Settings. See [Inventory](#/docs/inventory).

### Unlock

Entering your password so your encrypted data can be opened on this device. By default you are asked after each refresh, and Administrators can change that in Settings. See [Security](#/docs/security).

### User type

What a person on your team is allowed to do: Administrator, Standard or View. Also called a role. See [Team](#/docs/team).

### Username

Your own name for signing in, 3 to 30 letters, numbers, dots, underscores or dashes. It is used together with the Reseller ID. See [Getting started](#/docs/getting-started).

### View

A user type that can look at inventory, customers and sales but not change anything or record sales. See [Team](#/docs/team).

### Void

Cancels a sale made by mistake: its devices go back to available and the sale stays in your history marked Void, left out of revenue and profit totals. It is not a refund; for a customer sending goods back use Return or refund. See [Sales](#/docs/sales).

### Warranty

The period after a sale during which you stand behind a device. Periods are set in Settings, chosen at Quick sale, and shown on the receipt with the end date. See [Settings](#/docs/settings).

> Emails and alerts only work after the Email section is set up, using either direct sending or an SMTP gateway. The person who runs your site sets this up, so if a message you expect never arrives, ask them to check it.
