## What this page covers

Every account runs on one of three plans: **trial**, **paid** or **free**. The plan decides whether the account can be changed day to day, or whether it can only be looked at. This page explains each plan in plain words, what the small label in the top bar means, what "read-only" really involves, and what to do when a period runs out.

The most important thing to know is reassuring: **when a trial or paid period ends, nothing is deleted.** Your account stays exactly as it was. You can still sign in, look at everything and export it. What stops is making changes, until your plan continues.

> You do not pay or change plans inside the app. Your plan is set by your site administrator, the person or company that runs your myBoxStock site. To continue, contact them.

## The three plans

### Trial

A trial is a free, time-limited period to try the app with your real stock and customers. When you create your own account on the sign-up page, it tells you the number of free days (the standard length is 14 days, though your site administrator can choose another) and that no card is needed.

During a trial everything works normally: adding stock, making sales, adding people, changing settings. The trial has an end date, and the app counts down the days for you.

A site administrator can also give you a trial of a different length, or extend one that is still running.

### Paid

A paid plan is set by your site administrator once you have agreed terms with them. A paid plan may have a "paid through" date, in which case it works normally up to the end of that day. It may also have no end date, in which case it simply keeps working until your site administrator changes it. Everything works normally while the plan is active.

### Free

A free plan is a complimentary account that never expires. Your site administrator may use it for friends, partners or special arrangements. It works normally, always.

## The chip in the top bar

At the top of every page you can see your business name, a small coloured label (when one applies), your username and role, and a **Sign out** button. The small coloured label is the plan chip.

- **Free trial · N days left** (blue): you are on a trial. N counts down each day. When it is one day it says "1 day left". Use it as a reminder to decide before it runs out.
- **Trial ended — read-only** (red): your trial or your paid period has ended and the account is now read-only.
- **No chip**: you are on a paid plan that is in date, or on a free plan. Nothing needs your attention.

Note that the red chip uses the same words for an ended paid period as for an ended trial. It simply means "this account is read-only right now".

## What read-only means

Read-only is a pause on changes. The account is held safe and untouched.

### What still works

- **Signing in** and signing out.
- **Looking at everything**: Home, Inventory, Customers, Sales, receipts and Team.
- **Exporting everything.** On the [Security](#/docs/security) page, an Administrator can still click **Export** in the **Your data** card and download the zip file with all your spreadsheets. The export is built in your browser from your own data.
- **Changing your own password** and turning two-factor on or off.
- **Closing the account** if you decide to leave, which keeps its normal seven-day waiting period.
- **Reading this documentation.**

### What stops

Anything that changes the account is refused with a message that says: "This account is read-only because its trial or paid period has ended. Please contact support to continue." This includes:

- Adding or editing devices, models and customers.
- Making a sale, or voiding one.
- Changing settings.
- Adding, changing or removing people and resetting access.
- Changing your email address, creating a new recovery key, and signing devices out from the Activity page.

You may see the message appear as a red notice after clicking a button. Nothing was saved, and nothing was lost. If you were in the middle of something, copy down your details and continue when the plan is back.

### What read-only does not do

- It does not delete your data, now or later.
- It does not change your password, your recovery key or who is on your team.
- It does not make your data readable by anyone else. Your information is still encrypted and still only readable by you and your team.

## Why export during a trial

Even though nothing is deleted when a period ends, you should not rely on that alone. Your data is yours and you are responsible for keeping your own copies (see [Your data, your responsibility](#/docs/your-data-your-responsibility)). A sensible habit is to export before the trial ends, and again on the day it does. Then you hold a full copy whatever happens next.

1. Open **Security**.
2. In the **Your data** card, click **Export**.
3. Save the zip file somewhere safe, and open it to check it.

Only Administrators can use this button. If you are not an Administrator, ask one to do it.

## How to continue

When your trial or paid period is about to end, or has ended:

1. **Contact your site administrator.** Tell them your Reseller ID (shown at the top of the [Security](#/docs/security) page) and that you would like to continue.
2. They will set your plan: paid with an end date, paid without an end date, free, or a longer trial.
3. The change takes effect for your account straight away. Reload the page or sign in again, and the red chip disappears (or the blue one updates).
4. Carry on exactly where you left off: all your stock, customers and sales are still there.

Your site administrator may take payment in whatever way you arrange with them. The app does not collect card details.

## What happens at the end of a period

1. **Before the end**: during a trial the blue chip counts down the days.
2. **At the end**: the account becomes read-only. The chip turns red and says "Trial ended — read-only". If your account has an email address, the site sends a notice that the period has ended, and your site administrator is told too.
3. **After the end**: you can look and export, and you can ask to continue at any time. Nothing is removed because a period has ended.
4. **If you choose to close the account**: this is a separate action. See [Your data, your responsibility](#/docs/your-data-your-responsibility) for the seven-day waiting period and what is erased.

## Common questions

**Can my team still work after the trial ends?** They can sign in and look, but not change anything. A sale made at the counter would have to be written down on paper until the plan continues, then entered afterwards.

**Does a closing account behave the same way?** It is similar. A closing account is also read-only, and shows a red banner with the erase date and a **Restore account** button for Administrators. That is separate from the plan chip. See [Security](#/docs/security).

**I do not see any chip. Is something wrong?** No. A paid plan that is in date and a free plan show no chip.

**The chip says 0 days left.** The trial ends today, at the end of its last day. Export now and contact your site administrator.

**Can the site administrator read my data to check my plan?** No. The plan, dates and notes are about the account only. Your inventory, customers and sales stay unreadable to them.

**Who can see the plan chip?** Everyone on the account sees it in the top bar. Only your site administrator can change the plan.

## Tips

> Put the trial end date in your calendar the day you sign up, and decide a few days early whether to continue.

> Export before the trial ends, even if you plan to continue.

> If you are a staff member rather than the owner, tell the owner when you see the blue chip drop to a few days. Many people do not look at the top bar.
